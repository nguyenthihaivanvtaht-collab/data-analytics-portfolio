# Financial Analysis of Duc Giang Chemicals Group (DGC)

An individual course project analyzing the financial performance and position of Duc Giang Chemicals Group (DGC) from 2020 to 2024. Southern Basic Chemicals Joint Stock Company (CSV) was used as a benchmark for comparison.

## Project Objectives

- Assess DGC’s financial position, operating efficiency, liquidity, leverage, profitability, and cash flow.
- Compare selected financial indicators with CSV.
- Identify key performance trends and financial risks.

## Scope and Methods

The analysis used the companies’ financial statements for 2020–2024 and covered:

- Asset and capital structure
- Receivables and inventory efficiency
- Liquidity and debt ratios
- Interest coverage
- ROS, ROA, and ROE
- Working capital indicators
- Free cash flow to the firm (FCFF) and free cash flow to equity (FCFE)

Financial ratios and cash flow measures were calculated in Excel. The report also discusses industry outlook and DGC’s competitive position.

## Key Findings

- DGC had a larger operating scale and higher profitability and free cash flow than CSV during the period analyzed.
- DGC’s profitability and cash flow varied across years, reflecting changes in market conditions and chemical-product prices.
- The report discusses cost control, cash flow management, and investment planning as areas relevant to DGC’s continued performance.
