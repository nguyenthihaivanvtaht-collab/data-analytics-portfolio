# Amazon Scented Candle Market Research

A team market research project conducted during the Mega Digital practicum. The project explored the feasibility of launching a scented-candle product on Amazon using product, keyword, pricing, and customer-review data.

## Objectives

- Assess the Amazon scented-candle market and identify relevant competitors.
- Examine product listings, pricing, sales estimates, and customer feedback.
- Compare keyword search trends and identify seasonal patterns.
- Recommend product features and a potential launch price based on the research.

## Methodology

- Reviewed 10 selected Amazon scented-candle listings using Helium 10’s Chrome Extension (Xray) and available APIs.
- Analyzed listing prices, product characteristics, estimated sales, and customer reviews.
- Compared search-volume patterns across relevant keywords. For example, “jar candles set” showed steadier demand, while some gift-set-related keywords had more seasonal peaks.
- Compared competitor pricing, including listings from US- and China-based sellers, and summarized potential product positioning.

## Key Findings

- Search demand varied by keyword; some terms showed stronger seasonal patterns than others.
- Customer reviews helped identify product features and pain points to consider in a proposed product.
- China-based sellers in the reviewed sample often offered more competitive prices.
- The team proposed a launch price of approximately **$19**, compared with an estimated market average of approximately **$22**.

## Tools

- Helium 10 Chrome Extension (Xray)
- APIs
- Excel / Google Sheets

## Project Scope

This was a team research project and a simulated product-launch analysis. The findings and price are recommendations based on the data reviewed; the team did not launch the product or run Amazon advertising campaigns.
